The Single Most Publishable Idea Here

Not the architecture (a fairly standard small MLP) — the selection rule. Most federated learning papers pick "the best round by macro F1" and stop there, which is exactly the practice the earlier review flagged as needing disclosure. Formalizing rejection criteria that specifically target normal-only collapse, with a documented rationale, is a genuine, defensible methodological contribution worth a subsection of its own once Geo V2 has real results to report.

Before This Can Go In the Paper

  • Train at least one Geo V2 candidate through the full pipeline (audit → train → gate) and confirm it clears the hard constraints.
  • Report whether/how often candidates actually get rejected — an honest "N of M candidates passed the gate" number is more convincing than the gate's mere existence.
  • Run the three feature-variant ablation (Figure 11) before finalizing which variant is "the" Geo V2 result.
